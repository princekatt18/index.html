About

Katlego Joseph Chauke

Known as Prince Katt, I'm a graphic designer and brand creator from Lebowakgomo, Limpopo. Since 2011, I've been turning creative visions into wearable art and digital masterpieces.

Tsoga Mjita isn't just a store — it's a platform for the community. A place where creators, sellers, and dreamers from small towns get the same spotlight as the big city.

Every design I create carries the heartbeat of Limpopo. "Be the best to get the best."
